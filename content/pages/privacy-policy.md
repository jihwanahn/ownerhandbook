_Effective date: October 30, 2026. Last updated: October 30, 2026._

This Privacy Policy explains how The Owner's Handbook (https://ownerhandbook.blogspot.com),
published by Jihwan Ahn (JH Labs) ("we", "us"), handles information. Part A covers people
who read this website. Part B covers the private publishing app used to run it.

# Part A: Visitors to this website

## Who we are and the platform

This website is hosted on **Blogger**, a Google service. Google processes some data to
deliver the site, under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What is collected

- **Usage data.** Blogger's built-in statistics record visit statistics such as pages viewed, referrer,
  approximate location, and device and browser type. We see these only in aggregate.
- **Comments.** If you comment, Blogger stores the Google profile or name you use and
  your comment text. Comments are public.
- **Email.** If you write to us, we keep your message and address only as long as we
  need them to answer and keep a record of corrections.

You don't need an account to read this site, and no payments go through it. We don't
ask for sensitive personal information.

## Cookies

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. This site and Google use them:

- **to run the site:** Blogger needs them to serve pages and handle comments;
- **to measure traffic:** Blogger's built-in statistics only (no other analytics tool is
  installed);
- **for advertising:** see below.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Where required, visitors in
the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are shown a Google-certified consent message before
advertising cookies are used.

## Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site uses **Google AdSense** to show ads.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier
  visits to this website or other websites.
- Google's advertising cookies let Google and its partners serve ads to you based on
  your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
- You can turn off personalized advertising in
  [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). You can also opt out of some
  third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at
  [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- To see how Google uses information from sites that use its services, read
  [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- Google may also show non-personalized ads, which use cookies for frequency capping,
  aggregated reporting and fraud prevention.

## Your rights, including US state privacy laws

You can ask what personal information we hold about you (for example, an email you sent
or a comment), and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to the address below; we reply
within 30 days.

**California (CCPA/CPRA) and other US states.** Residents of California and several
other states have rights to know, access, correct and delete personal information, and
to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context
behavioral advertising. We don't sell personal information for money. Advertising
cookies set by Google may count as "sharing" under these laws. You can opt out by
turning off personalized ads in Google Ads Settings, by using a browser that sends a
Global Privacy Control signal, or by contacting us. We won't treat you differently for
using these rights.

**EEA/UK visitors** have rights under the GDPR, including access, correction, deletion
and objection, and can complain to their local data protection authority.

## Children's privacy

This website is written for adults running a business. It isn't directed at children
under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a
child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Links to other sites

The guides link to the IRS, state agencies and other sites. Their own privacy policies
apply when you visit them.

# Part B: The JHL Publisher app and Google user data

## The Publisher App and Google user data

This website is written, edited, and published by its owner using a private, single-user application — JHL Publisher. The app is not distributed to the public, has exactly one user (the site owner), and is not used by any third party. It connects to the site owner's own Google Account using Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization.

**What Google user data the app accesses** (only from the site owner's account):

- **Blogger API v3** (authorization scope *https://www.googleapis.com/auth/blogger*) — to read and manage the posts, pages, and comments of this website: creating drafts, submitting them for the owner's review, publishing approved articles, and maintaining existing pages.
- **Search Console API, read-only** (authorization scope *https://www.googleapis.com/auth/webmasters.readonly*) — to view search performance and indexing status for this website's own pages.

**How the data is used.** Solely to draft, review, publish, update, and maintain articles on this website, and to measure how its pages perform in search. The data is used only to provide these user-facing publishing features — no other purpose.

**How the data is stored.** OAuth credentials (client ID and refresh token) are stored as local files on the site owner's own computer, protected by the operating system's account controls. Article drafts and related working files are also kept locally, and reach this website only after the owner's review. The Publisher App does not operate any server of its own and does not send Google user data to any third-party service. All communication with Google's APIs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

**Sharing.** Google user data accessed by the Publisher App is **not shared, sold, rented, or transferred** to any third party, is not used for advertising, for ad personalization, or to train generalized machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models, and is not read by any human other than the site owner.

**Limited Use.** The Publisher App's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

**Retention and deletion.** Access credentials remain valid until revoked. The site owner (or any user of a connected Google service) can revoke the app's access at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), which immediately ends its access to Google data. Google user data can also be deleted by contacting us at the address at the end of this policy; such requests are honored within 30 days.
