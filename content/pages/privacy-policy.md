This policy explains what The Owner's Handbook collects, why, and what you can do
about it. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

## What is collected

- **Server and analytics data.** Aggregate information about visits: pages viewed,
  referrer, approximate location, device and browser type.
- **Comments.** If you comment, Blogger stores the name or profile you provide and
  your comment text. Comments are public.
- **Email.** If you write to the address on the Contact page, that message is kept
  until the question is answered.

No account is created to read this site, and no payment details pass through it.

## Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files a site can store in your browser. This site uses them for
three purposes:

- **Essential / platform cookies** — Blogger (Google) needs these to serve the site
  and keep comment forms working.
- **Analytics cookies** — to measure which guides are read and which are not.
- **Advertising cookies** — set by Google and its partners when ads are shown (see
  below), used to select and measure advertising.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking advertising
cookies does not stop ads being shown; it makes them less relevant.

## Google advertising (AdSense / third-party vendors)

This site intends to display advertising served by **Google AdSense** and its partner
networks.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior
  visits to this site and other sites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you
  based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
- You can opt out of personalised advertising in **Google Ads Settings**
  (adssettings.google.com), or opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for
  personalised advertising at **aboutads.info**.
- Google's own practices are described in Google's Privacy & Terms.

## Analytics

If Google Analytics (or an equivalent) is enabled, it records aggregated visit data.
IP addresses are not used to build a personal profile of you.

## Your choices and rights

- Ask what is held about you, and ask for it to be deleted, via the Contact page.
- California residents have specific rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right
  to know and to delete; the Contact page is the route to exercise them.
- Comment removal requests are honoured on request.

## Children

This site is aimed at adults running a business and is not directed at children
under 13.

## Changes to this policy

Material changes are dated at the top of the page. Continuing to use the site after a
change means you accept the updated policy.
